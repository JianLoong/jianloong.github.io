Conway's Game of Life is a classic cellular automaton where simple rules create fascinating patterns. Try the interactive demo below to see how patterns evolve!

## How to Use
- Click cells to toggle them alive or dead
- Press **Randomize** to fill the grid
- Press **Start** to run, **Stop** to pause, **Clear** to reset
- Adjust speed and grid size as you like
