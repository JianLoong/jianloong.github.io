# Traffic Intersection Simulator 🚦

This post demonstrates a realistic, interactive traffic intersection simulator using React and Astro Islands. Start the simulation to see how the lights change for each direction at a two-way intersection, and how cars behave in response!

<TrafficIntersectionSimulator client:visible />

## Features & Improvements

- **Accurate car logic:**
  - Cars stop at the stop line and never enter the intersection on red.
  - Cars maintain a minimum gap and never overlap.
  - Only one car per direction is allowed in the intersection at a time.
  - Cars in the intersection finish crossing even if the light changes.
  - Cars despawn only after leaving the visible area.
  - Maximum of 6 cars per direction to prevent overcrowding.
  - Cars spawn at a realistic rate and queue up closely before the stop line.
  - North-South and East-West cars have distinct colors for clarity.

## How it works

The simulator models a basic two-way intersection with North-South and East-West traffic lights. Only one direction is green at a time, with yellow and red phases for safe transitions. Cars obey the lights, stop at the correct place, and queue up realistically. The SVG rendering provides a clear, visually appealing view of the intersection and traffic flow.
