Social card for jianloong.github.io, 1200x630.

PRODUCED, not generated by an image model. tools/og-card.mjs builds the card as
HTML in the site's own design system and renders it with the Chrome already on
the machine; the committed PNG is the browser's render of that markup. Re-run
with `npm run og`.

THE WORLD. The site's atlas plate. Ground #0a0d18 (the sky), ruled with the same
96px coordinate grid the pages carry, in #1b2138. Ink #e9e7db. Hairline #232a42.
Type is the site's own faces, loaded from assets/fonts: Spectral 500 for the
title, Azeret Mono 400 for the legend and the footer. No image model was used
and no third-party asset appears in the card.

ELEMENTS. A mono legend carrying a magnitude mark whose tint is the entry's
reading-time band — #f0c674 at 8 minutes and over, #8fb8c9 at 6 and over,
#b892c9 below — the title in Spectral, and a footer rule with the site URL and a
mono label. The banding is the same one the catalogue uses, so a mark on a card
means what a mark on the site means.

COPY is the site's own, not invented. The default card carries the homepage h1
verbatim; each post card carries that post's title verbatim, with its real
category and reading time in the legend.
